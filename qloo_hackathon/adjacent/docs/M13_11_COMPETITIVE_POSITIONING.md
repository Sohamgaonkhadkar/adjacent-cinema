# M13.11 — Competitive Positioning

Research dates: vidIQ/TubeBuddy/Viewstats first surveyed 2026-10-10 (M13.9 research,
`M13_9_CREATOR_INTELLIGENCE_RESEARCH.md`), re-confirmed from those notes 2026-10-11;
MUBI surveyed fresh 2026-10-11. Claims are labelled [FACT] (in cited source),
[MARKETING] (vendor claim, unverified), [INFERENCE] (our reading).

## 1. The tools

### vidIQ (vidiq.com)
- [FACT] YouTube SEO/optimization suite: keyword research, channel audit, competitor
  tracking, AI Coach; Free tier (150 AI credits) to ~$79/mo; AI-credit metering.
- [INFERENCE] Unit of analysis is the video/keyword/channel. No cultural-affinity data;
  no hypothesis→experiment→evaluation lifecycle.

### TubeBuddy (tubebuddy.com)
- [FACT] YouTube workflow toolkit; A/B thumbnail-and-title testing is the flagship,
  gated at the top tier; Free–$49/mo; per-channel licensing.
- [INFERENCE] Its experiments are packaging A/B tests run after publication. No
  pre-release positioning hypotheses; no evidence trail attached to a decision.

### Viewstats (viewstats.com)
- [FACT] Built by MrBeast's team; outlier detection across channels, thumbnail history
  database; Free–$249+/mo.
- [INFERENCE] Analytics-first: describes what already happened; no cultural context for
  a not-yet-released concept.

### MUBI (mubi.com)
- [FACT] Arthouse streaming service (founded 2007, London); human-curated rotating
  catalogue, editorial context (Notebook journal), MUBI Go cinema tickets, own
  production/distribution arm; ~€12–15/mo (multiple 2026 reviews).
- [FACT] Curation is explicitly human/editorial, "not algorithms" — positioning built
  on curated cultural framing for VIEWERS.
- [INFERENCE] MUBI validates that curated cultural framing is a valued product
  principle — but its primary user is the audience, not the filmmaker. It offers a
  creator no decision tooling: no positioning hypotheses, no experiments, no
  outcome records.

## 2. Comparison

| Dimension | vidIQ | TubeBuddy | Viewstats | MUBI | **Adjacent** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary user | YouTuber | YouTuber | YouTuber/analyst | Film viewer | **Indie filmmaker / short-film creator** |
| Core problem | rank & package videos | workflow + A/B packaging | explain past performance | watch curated cinema | **position a film in its cultural world and learn from release** |
| Data sources | YouTube SEO/stats | YouTube | YouTube analytics | licensed catalogue + editors | **Qloo cross-domain cultural affinity (+ creator's own YouTube metrics, authorized)** |
| Cultural understanding | none (keywords) | none | none | deep but human-only, viewer-facing | **measured cross-domain affinity, per-claim provenance** |
| Explainability | score-based | minimal | outlier stats | editorial essays | **every claim cites the API call that produced it; FACT vs INTERPRETATION separated** |
| Testable hypotheses | no | A/B packaging only | no | no | **falsifiable Positioning Bet + Test Card** |
| Outcome recording/learning | no | partial (A/B results) | no | no | **append-only experiment ledger: observations (zero≠missing) → evaluations that supersede** |
| Qloo-grounded | no | no | no | no | **yes — the differentiator** |

## 3. Defensible position (and its limits)

**Position:** the only reviewed product that connects *measured cultural evidence*
(Qloo) to a *falsifiable positioning hypothesis*, a *saved experiment*, and an
*append-only evaluation record* — for filmmakers, before and after release.

**Honest limits:**
- vidIQ/TubeBuddy/Viewstats are mature, funded, and better at SEO/packaging mechanics.
  Adjacent deliberately does not compete there.
- MUBI proves demand for curated cultural framing but serves viewers; Adjacent serves
  creators making positioning decisions. They are complements, not rivals.
- Adjacent's live Qloo path is credential-dependent and not yet live-verified in this
  environment; its evidence quality is bounded by Qloo's data model (affinity ≠
  viewership — enforced in-product).
- "No reviewed tool has the decision-lifecycle" is a verified-absence claim over the
  reviewed set, not proof no such product exists anywhere.
